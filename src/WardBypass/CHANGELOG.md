# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-16

- Initial version: client-side bypass of player-built wards with an F11 toggle. Location wards and creature terrain damage stay vanilla.
- Ward locator (F10): markers over every nearby player-built ward, visible through walls and terrain, with owner, distance and state; wards outside the view are pinned to the screen edge with an arrow, and the ground ring of nearby enabled wards is shown. Each marker is a pin whose dot sits on the centre of the ward stone, and markers stay on the ward at render scales below 100 %.
